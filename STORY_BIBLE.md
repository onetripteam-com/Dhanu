# MANIFESTO — story bible and continuity

## Core premise
One minute before Dhanu's birthday, time freezes. A letter allegedly written by Dhanu one year from now leads him to Manifesto, a legendary timepiece. It is not a shortcut to success; its mechanisms echo the choices and relationships that shape him.

The four guiding values are freedom, discipline, courage, and connection. Tone: an original cinematic anime graphic novel with occasional dry humour, suspense and a sincere birthday payoff. Dhanu—not his friends—is the protagonist.

## Cast
- **Dhanu:** Hero. Original anime-inspired navy expedition coat, off-white shirt, red sash, dark tousled hair. Keep his face, clothing details and palette consistent across all future poses and emotions. No name editor. His approaching graduation is part of the emotional ending.
- **Nix:** Copper-ringed storm petrel; sharp-tongued guide with a white throat and charcoal wings.
- **Sanjay:** Best friend, occasional reliable voice of reason and radio assistance. Cameo in Chapter 2. Do not make the story about him.
- **Siri:** Dhanu's girlfriend. If introduced, let her have a plot-relevant, grounded moment. Her real-world connection must not become a rescue cliché.
- **Praju, Manu, Aisha, Uha:** Supporting friends available for later brief, purposeful references; avoid token cameos.
- **The other Dhanu:** A possibility trapped by an unmade choice in the Sea of Almosts. His warning sets up the cost of Manifesto.

## Existing chapters — locked major beats
1. **Eleven Fifty-Nine.** 11:59 p.m. Rain and city clocks freeze. Nix delivers a sealed letter in Dhanu's own handwriting dated a year in the future. The route to the old harbour follows moving water. A flagless ferry awaits; a stranger has Dhanu's face.
2. **The Ship of Forgotten Tomorrows.** Sea of Almosts filled with ships abandoned by unrealised decisions. An impossible radio message from Sanjay reveals which beacon is real. The stranger is one possible Dhanu who never departed. Manifesto's first gear is **BEGIN**. A mountain temple appears.
3. **The Thousandth Morning.** A bellkeeper has struck a jammed bell 999 times. Dhanu discovers a brace and crooked counterweight; patient observation solves what brute repetition cannot. Manifesto's second gear is **RETURN**. The last second of 11:59 comes back, but not the missing minute. The letter now suggests the minute was **promised**, not stolen. A walled city appears.

## Future outline — not yet implemented
4. **The City Behind the Wall.** Noir-scale walled city. The city guards memory and promises; the apparent theft is revealed as a debt or voluntary pledge. Make Siri's clue meaningful and let Dhanu solve the central decision himself. A carefully designed investigation or evidence puzzle could fit. Optional brief Sanjay role, if earned by the plot. Strong original art direction and consistent action/expression poses.
5. **The Hour You Choose.** Resolution of who promised the minute and why. The ending connects his birthday, bonds, personal agency, and his nearing graduation. Payoff should be warm, human, not preachy.

## Experience rules
- A premium **reader-first** vertical graphic novel; not a generic site with cards.
- Generate consistent multi-angle/multi-expression artwork for Dhanu, preserving his recognizable silhouette and costume. Use character images at turning points, not pasted everywhere.
- A challenge must reveal character, unlock a plot fact or deliver a meaningful choice; challenges are optional for reading accessibility.
- Touch-first, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion safe. Maintain offline behaviour without third-party tracking.
- Use original characters and artwork. Inspirational themes can nod to One Piece, Naruto, Attack on Titan and Batman Begins, but do not copy character likenesses, outfits or plot beats.
